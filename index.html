The Stay

Where the canopy meets the coastline

Wet Sand sits beneath mature jungle canopy on one of the best-located lots in Santa Teresa — close enough to hear the surf, shaded enough to nap through the afternoon heat.

Wraparound balconies catch the breeze off the Pacific. Ceiling fans spin over every terrace, howler monkeys pass through the trees at dawn, and the sand is a barefoot stroll down the lane.

Rinse off the salt, grab a cold drink, and watch the canopy turn gold at sunset from the covered rooftop lounge.

The Neighborhood

Santa Teresa, at your doorstep

This stretch of the Nicoya Peninsula packs world-class surf, food, and jungle into one barefoot beach town — and Wet Sand sits right in the middle of it.

Surf

Consistent breaks, year-round

Playa Santa Teresa serves up some of Costa Rica's most reliable waves — beginner-friendly rollers at mid-tide, punchier peaks for the experienced. Board rentals and surf schools are minutes away.

Sunsets

The nightly main event

The whole town faces west. Every evening the beach fills with people watching the sky catch fire over the Pacific — or skip the crowd and watch from the rooftop.

Food

A genuinely great food scene

Santa Teresa's restaurant strip is steps from the villa — wood-fired pizza, fresh-catch ceviche, smoothie bowls, sushi, and beach bars, all walkable in flip-flops.

Jungle

Wildlife included

Howler monkeys, iguanas, and toucans are regulars in the trees around the villa. Waterfall hikes at Montezuma and the Cabo Blanco reserve are an easy day trip.
